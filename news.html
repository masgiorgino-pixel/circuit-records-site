Release party

RAW FREQUENCIES — SESSION 01

After several months dedicated to the birth of our new independent label, we are finally back live with the first official Circuit Records night, presenting our first release: CIRCUIT 01.

A night dedicated to tekno tribe sounds and pure underground energy.

For the occasion, we will be joined by NIK23 and ZE PAR, two artists highly active in the underground free party scene, known for their high-energy live sets and for several collaborations and productions, including vinyl releases.

Line-up: 3 live sets + 1 DJ set — NIK23 / SBUME / SUSHI / ZE PAR.

Vinyl, merch and gadgets will be available during the night. Small gifts for the first arrivals.

Entry: 5 Euro all night. Barrio’s Live — Piazza Donne Partigiane, Milan. Friday 12.06.26 — 00:00 / 05:00–05:30.
