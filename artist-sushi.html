SUSHI

Sushi started producing and mixing electronic music around 2007, mainly focusing on a strictly underground hardcore sound, heavily influenced by late 90s/early 00s industrial and frenchcore releases.

During the years spent throwing parties in parks around Milan, he matured a sincere love for soundsystem design and built his own one, which later became a signature in Circuit Gang's parties after he joined the crew in 2015.

Nowadays you can find him playing Neurofunk DnB, Industrial Hardcore, and everything that comes out when they collide, but you might as well witness him dropping 90's HipHop bangers, or Funky and Disco classics.
